## Conversion surfaces

Every commercial page should have at least one primary conversion path:

1. Calculate system
2. Request estimate
3. Call hotline

The calculator result should transition naturally into the progressive lead form.

## Route rules

- Slugs are lowercase, Vietnamese-friendly and stable.
- No duplicate pages for the same search intent.
- Each indexable page gets unique title, description, canonical URL and structured data where appropriate.
- Paid landing pages may use focused variants but must not create thin SEO duplicates.
